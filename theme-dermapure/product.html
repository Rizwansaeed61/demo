Directions with Water

Apply a generous amount of cleanser to the skin and gently massage in small circular motions. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water and pat dry with a soft, clean towel.

Pro Tip: Ideal for sensitive reactive skin prone to redness after tap water contact.

Directions without Water

Apply a generous quantity of cleanser to the skin and rub gently. Remove excess with a soft facial cotton tissue, leaving a thin transparent hydrating film on the skin surface.

Post-Procedure: Safe for post-laser or chemical peel patients requiring waterless cleansing.

Key Medical Actives

Glycerin: Powerful humectant that attracts and locks environmental moisture into outer epidermis cells.
Niacinamide (Vitamin B3): Enhances ceramide synthesis, reduces erythema, and calms reactive inflammation.
Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5): Accelerates natural epidermal re-epithelialization and tissue repair.
