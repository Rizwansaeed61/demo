## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, clinical-yet-approachable dermatological science ethos. The brand aesthetic strikes a balance between pharmaceutical-grade credibility and everyday personal care warmth, inspired by iconic gentle skincare staples. It establishes immediate clinical authority through crisp, high-clarity layouts while eliminating sterile, intimidating medical rigidity via gentle organic greens, soft pill geometry, and reassuring typography.

The target audience encompasses consumers seeking gentle, dermatologist-backed skincare solutions—from patients managing sensitive, reactive skin conditions (eczema, rosacea, acne) to everyday wellness enthusiasts prioritizing barrier repair and safety.

Key stylistic principles:
- **Clinical Precision:** Crisp borders, clear micro-copy, transparent ingredient communication, and structured scientific proof points.
- **Gentle Tactility:** Generous whitespace, pill-shaped tags, soft pastel tinted highlight containers, and organic rounded CTAs.
- **Trust-First Architecture:** Prominent dermatologist recommendation seals, clinical trial metric ribbons, and clear dermatological claim iconography (e.g., Fragrance Free, Hypoallergenic).

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative medical presence grounded by deep clinical navy blue, balanced with the vitality and restorative nature of leaf green and refreshing sky tints.

- **Primary (`#004380` - Medical Navy Blue):** Commands authority, trust, and scientific pedigree. Used for dominant headings, authoritative brand seals, high-emphasis interactive states, and top banner utility bars.
- **Secondary (`#6cb33f` - Gentle Botanical Lime):** Communicates active skin restoration, gentle botanical soothing, and positive clinical efficacy. Applied to high-conversion CTA gradients, highlight ribbons, and active routine indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7` - Clinical Aqua / Sky Blue):** Signals deep hydration, cellular moisture, and hypoallergenic water purity. Used for active selection pills, selected radio borders, and secondary informational accents.
- **Tints & Washes:** 
  - Mint Surface Tint: `#f4fbf0` and `#eef7e8` for trial stat highlights and positive routine steps.
  - Hydration Sky Tint: `#e0f2fe` for hydration pill badges and subtle backdrop frames.
  - Neutral Base: Pure clinical white `#ffffff` canvas with subtle hairline borders (`#e5e7eb` / `#f3f4f6`) to maintain surgical purity without harshness.
- **Body & Text:** `#1e293b` (Slate 800) for high-contrast, effortless legibility, and `#64748b` (Slate 500) for secondary dermatological annotations and ratings.

## Typography

The typography leverages `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all tiers to merge rigorous modern geometry with subtle organic warmth. The slightly rounded terminals evoke approachability, essential for de-escalating patient anxiety around clinical skincare.

Hierarchy rules:
- **Product Titles & Display:** Set in Bold (`700`) with tight letter spacing for impactful readability in commerce grids.
- **Clinical Proof & Benefit Claims:** Medium to Semibold weights ensure clinical results (e.g., "94% users felt instant hydration") stand out clearly against secondary background tints.
- **Numbers & E-commerce Figures:** Numbers, volume measures (e.g., `125ml`, `250ml`), and price tags maintain tabular alignment with prominent optical weights to ensure unambiguous pricing clarity.
- **Skin Type Badges:** Uppercase or Title Case with extra tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) in small compact formats to allow dense, tidy classification tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an 8px base rhythm with 4px sub-increments for micro-components such as badge padding, checkbox alignments, and clinical icon labels.

- **Mobile Viewport (320px - 640px):** Single-column fluid view with standard `16px` (`1rem`) side margins. Product image carousels utilize compact squared aspect ratios with horizontal miniature pagination thumbnails below. Secondary modules (Size Selector, Claim Icons, Complete Routine Cards) stack vertically with `16px` to `24px` section dividers.
- **Tablet & Desktop Viewport (768px - 1152px+):** A 12-column layout. In product detail contexts, an asymmetric split-screen layout places sticky media galleries on the left (5 columns) and clinical narrative, size selectors, claims, and routines on the right (7 columns).
- **Rhythm Rules:** Vertical spacing between major clinical sections is consistently `32px` to prevent visual fatigue, while tight `8px` and `12px` gaps encapsulate closely related sub-options (e.g., size selection pills or benefit checklist items).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built on clinical cleanliness using flat surface separation and whisper-light structural outlines rather than heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows.

- **Surface Layers:**
  - `Base`: Pure white (`#ffffff`).
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#f8fafc` for technical specifications or routine tracker wrappers.
  - `Surface Tint Positive`: Soft mint tint (`#f4fbf0`) for user efficacy callouts.
  - `Surface Tint Hydration`: Gentle sky tint (`#f0f9ff`) for active selection highlights.
- **Borders & Outlines:** Primary depth is created using delicate 1px neutral borders (`#e2e8f0` or `#e5e7eb`). Active selected cards transition to a 1.5px border in Primary Medical Navy (`#004380`) or clinical blue.
- **Shadows:** Restricted to floating interactions:
  - Sticky Cart Bar / Floating Navigation: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 67, 128, 0.08)`.
  - Hover on Interactive Step Cards: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 59, 96, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a predominantly pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`) and softly sculpted vocabulary to emulate ergonomic skincare packaging, bottles, and gentle skin safety.

- **Pill Badges (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Used exclusively for dermatological attributes (`Sensitive Skin`, `Dry Skin`, `Acne Prone`), size selector tags, step badges (`Step 01`), and primary call-to-action buttons.
- **Component Enclosures (`12px` to `16px`):** Large cards, routine sequence containers, and product thumbnail frames employ balanced medium-curved corners that soften technical information without losing clinical rigor.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio selections with crisp cyan interior pips, and gently rounded squares (`4px` radius) for routine cross-sell selection.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Cart"):** Full-width pill shape (`rounded-full`). Features an energetic, clean horizontal gradient flowing from Medical Navy (`#004380`) to Active Botanical Green (`#6cb33f`). Text is uppercase, bold, white, with centered vertical alignment and smooth micro-scale on hover.
- **Secondary Routine Button:** Solid Medical Navy (`#004380`) pill with high-contrast white text, presenting bundle pricing directly inside the CTA (e.g., `ADD 2 ITEMS TO BAG | ₹710`).
- **Variant Selector Buttons:** Large pill/squircle cards containing variant specs (`125ml`, `₹335`). Unselected: White background, `#e5e7eb` border. Selected: `#f0f9ff` background with `#004380` active outline and a pre-checked radio element. Includes a pill-shaped discount badge (`10% OFF`) pinned to the top right.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- **Skin Concern Pills:** Micro-pill badges with neutral soft gray borders or subtle pastel fills (`#f1f5f9` or `#eef7e8`). Typography is small, crisp, and high-contrast.
- **Routine Step Badges:** Vibrant Lime Green (`#6cb33f`) background with white bold sans typography (e.g., `Step 01`, `Step 02`), establishing clear skincare sequence guidance.
- **Trial Metric Banner:** Soft green pill strip (`#f4fbf0` background with `#6cb33f` accent dot) highlighting clinical survey results: "94% users felt the product gently cleansed their skin."

### Skincare Claim Icons
- Circular monoline badges (`44px × 44px`) framed by a gentle 1px slate ring (`#cbd5e1`). Features minimal, clear outline iconography representing dermatological claims: Fragrance Free, Paraben Free, Hypoallergenic, and Deep Cleans. Accompanied by centered `11px` body labels below.

### Cards & Routine Modules
- **Product Routine Card:** Flat white background card bounded by a 1px `#e5e7eb` border. Comprises:
  1. Compact square thumbnail (`64px × 64px`) set on a subtle soft gray backdrop.
  2. Pill badges indicating targeted skin types.
  3. Bold title with a single-line clinical efficacy description.
  4. Step pill indicator positioned in the top-right corner.
  5. Interactive checkbox toggle paired with clear item price.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Primary Medical Navy or Vibrant Green active states with smooth transitions. Checkboxes within routine modules use a subtle border when inactive and solid `#004380` fill with a crisp white checkmark when selected.
